# Assets Folder

## 📸 Add Your Profile Photo

To display your professional photo on the portfolio:

1. **Save your photo** as `profile.jpg` in this folder
2. **Recommended dimensions**: 800x800 pixels or larger (square works best)
3. **Format**: JPG or PNG
4. **File name**: Must be exactly `profile.jpg`

## Your Photos

You have two professional photos:
- Use either photo from your attachments
- Resize if needed to around 800x800 pixels
- Save as `profile.jpg` in this assets folder

Once added, refresh your browser to see the photos appear!
